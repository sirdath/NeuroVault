# NeuroVault Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to NeuroVault.

This agreement exists so the project can keep its promises. It is short, and it
is designed to take from you as little as possible while still letting the
project defend and distribute your work.

By submitting a contribution (a pull request, patch, or any other work), you
agree to the terms below for that contribution and every contribution you make
to this project.

## 1. You keep your copyright

**You retain full ownership of your contribution.** Nothing here transfers or
assigns your copyright. You can reuse your own work anywhere, for anything,
forever.

## 2. What you grant

You grant the project maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, reproduce, modify, publicly display,
sublicense, and distribute your contribution and works derived from it —
including the right to **relicense it under a different licence**, and to
distribute it as part of a commercial or dual-licensed edition.

You also grant a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
irrevocable patent licence covering any patent claims you own that your
contribution necessarily infringes, for making, using, selling, and
distributing the project. This grant terminates for anyone who files a patent
claim alleging that the project infringes their patents.

## 3. Why the relicensing right is here

Because it cannot be added later. Every project that has ever needed to change
its licence — to add a patent grant, to adopt a stronger copyleft, to offer a
commercial edition, or to move under a foundation — needed permission from
every past contributor to do it. VLC spent three years collecting those
permissions and still had to rewrite the code of one contributor who never
replied.

Asking now costs you a checkbox. Not asking would mean the project can never
change its licence again, no matter how good the reason.

**This is not a plan to close the source.** See the commitments below.

## 4. What the project commits to in return

These commitments are also written in [CORE-COVENANT.md](CORE-COVENANT.md) and
are binding on the maintainer:

- **The local core stays under an OSI-approved open-source licence.** Today
  that is MIT. It will not become proprietary.
- **NeuroVault works fully offline, with no account and no remote kill
  switch.** Local operation is never gated behind a paid tier.
- **Your notes stay yours** — durable, readable Markdown you can take
  elsewhere. Export, backup, deletion and restore are never paywalled.
- **No selling or model-training on vault data.** Ever.

You confirm that:

- the contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it

- if your employer has rights to work you create, you have permission to make
  the contribution, or your employer has waived those rights;
- to your knowledge, your contribution does not violate anyone else's rights;
- you are not knowingly including third-party code without identifying it and
  its licence in the pull request.

## 6. No warranty

Your contribution is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. You are not
expected to provide support for it.

## 7. How to sign

You do not need to send anything. The first time you open a pull request, a bot
will comment asking you to confirm. Reply with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

That's it. Your signature is recorded once and covers all your future
contributions to this project.
